## Event Details

The first Lyon NLP Connect meetup of the 2026–2027 academic year will take place on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

The meetup will include:

* Short presentations by participants about their research topics
* Birds-of-a-Feather (BoF) discussions on:

  * LLM-based agents (usage and research)
  * Multitasks LLMs
* Collaborative discussion workshop

Participants who wish to present their work are invited to prepare a few slides covering the problem definition, research questions, and/or a summary of completed or ongoing work.

If you have a project idea for collaboration, you may also prepare a short description outlining the project and its current stage. Possible types of projects include, but are not limited to:

* Developing a new package or demo
* Participating in a competition or shared task
* Conducting a research experiment
* Starting or developing a collaborative research project
