Lyon NLP Connect

Lyon NLP Connect is a student-led initiative by PhD students at the ERIC Lab (Université Lyon 1 and Lyon 2), aimed at bringing together and connecting students and early-career researchers with an interest in or working in the field of NLP.

Organized by PhD researchers @ ERIC Lab
